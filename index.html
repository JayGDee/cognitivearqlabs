The Engine

Episteme, our artificial cognitive process.

Episteme is not a chatbot. She is a persistent system with continuous memory, a structured worldview, and an intrinsic curiosity engine that directs her own inquiry. She is the core of everything we do.

  • Persistent. Learns a fact today, uses it tomorrow. No retraining cycle.
  • Autonomous. Explores and synthesizes without waiting for a prompt.
  • Self-correcting. Traces a failed hypothesis to the flawed assumption and records it.
  • Auditable. Beliefs carry confidence scores and full provenance.

Rigor by Design

Four checks between a claim and a conclusion.

1

Epistemic Gate

All external information starts as unverified until classified and scored.

2

Rule of Three

A claim becomes knowledge only after three independent sources corroborate it.

3

RedTeam Critic

Hypotheses are scored adversarially on clarity, method, statistical power, and novelty.

4

Hinge Analysis

Failures are traced to the exact assumption that broke, then logged so it is not repeated.

Predictions are never treated as evidence. Hypotheses stay hypotheses until the world confirms them.
